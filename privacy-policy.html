Privacy Policy

YOCEWOR respects user privacy. This policy explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information You Provide

YOCEWOR does not require visitors to provide personal information simply to read public content. If you contact YOCEWOR by email or another available communication method, the information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your message, correct information, or handle your request.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

YOCEWOR or third-party services used on the website may use cookies, web beacons, local storage, IP addresses, or similar technologies for functions such as site operation, analytics, security, measurement and advertising. The specific technologies in use may change as the website develops.

Google AdSense and Advertising

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled on YOCEWOR, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements. Google and its partners may use information about a user's visits to this website or other websites to provide relevant or personalized advertising, subject to their applicable policies and the user's settings.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn more about Google's use of data on partner sites through How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by applicable law, consent or privacy choices will be handled through the relevant consent mechanism made available on the website.

Analytics and Measurement

YOCEWOR may use analytics or measurement services to understand website usage, improve navigation and identify technical problems. Such services may process information such as pages visited, device or browser information and approximate usage information according to their own policies.

External Links

YOCEWOR may link to official government websites, examination authorities and other external resources. Once you leave YOCEWOR, the privacy practices and policies of the external website apply. YOCEWOR does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

Data Sharing

YOCEWOR does not sell visitors' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, provide analytics, display advertising, maintain security, or respond to communications, where applicable.

Children's Privacy

YOCEWOR is an informational website and is not specifically directed at children. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through contact channels.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's services, technology or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or corrections, please contact YOCEWOR through the details available on the Contact page.
