Privacy & Safety
Your safety, privacy and control matter on YOCEWOR.
Community Rules
1
Treat other people with respect. Harassment, bullying and targeted abuse are not allowed.
2
Do not post illegal, dangerous, threatening or harmful content.
3
Do not impersonate another person or intentionally mislead other users.
4
Do not share private personal information belonging to another person without permission.
5
Do not use spam, scams, malicious links, automated abuse or deceptive activity.
6
Respect intellectual property and only upload media you are permitted to use.
7
Content involving sexual exploitation of minors, abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited.
Privacy Controls
You control important parts of your account. Use the settings system to choose account visibility and who can contact you. Avoid posting passwords, financial information, private addresses or other sensitive information publicly.
Account Safety
Use a strong unique password, keep your email secure, never share login credentials and sign out of devices you do not recognize.
