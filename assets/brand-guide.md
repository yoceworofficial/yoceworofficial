# YOCEWOR Brand System

**Brand:** YOCEWOR  
**Meaning:** Your Voice. Your World.  
**Tagline:** Your Voice. Your World.

## Core direction
Simple, premium, friendly, social-first. Avoid AI-demo aesthetics, excessive glow, and heavy gradients.

## Core colors
- Primary blue: `#1769FF`
- Deep ink: `#101828`
- Muted text: `#667085`
- Soft blue: `#B9D2FF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Logo assets
- `assets/yocewor-logo-mark.svg` — square app/logo mark
- `assets/yocewor-brand.svg` — wordmark + tagline lockup
- `assets/yocewor-favicon.svg` — favicon

## Usage
Use the mark for compact/mobile surfaces and the lockup for welcome, landing, and desktop brand areas. Keep clear space around the logo and do not recreate or distort it.
