Privacy Notice

YOCEWOR uses account, profile, content and technical information needed to operate the service, protect users, prevent abuse and provide requested features.

What we use

  • Account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Profile information you choose to publish.
  • Posts, comments, follows, messages and other content needed for the features you use.
  • Security, device and diagnostic information needed to prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.

Choices and controls

Privacy controls should let you manage public/private profile visibility and other sharing choices where the feature supports them. You should not share passwords or OTPs with anyone.

Security and retention

Access controls, database policies and security monitoring are used to protect data. Information is retained only as needed for the service, safety, legal obligations and dispute handling, subject to applicable law.

Indian data-protection framework

YOCEWOR will update its privacy practices to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection framework and applicable rules as they become applicable to the service. Required notices, consent/choice mechanisms, user rights and grievance processes will be implemented as applicable.
